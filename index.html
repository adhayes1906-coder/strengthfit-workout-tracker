Back-Safe Reminders

Avoid heavy barbell squats, heavy deadlifts, sit-ups, twisting crunches and high-impact running. Stop any movement that causes sharp pain, numbness or pain down the leg.
Use controlled reps Keep your spine neutral and move slowly. No jerking or twisting.

Workout Plan

3 days / week

Revised plan: leg press once weekly, varied chest work, and direct biceps and triceps work every day. Train on nonconsecutive days. Each session: 5 min warm-up + 27 min strength and core + 8 min cardio + 5 min stretching.

How to progress: Leave about two controlled reps available. Rest 45–75 seconds between sets, longer if needed. Add one rep per set within the listed range; once every set reaches the top comfortably, use the smallest available weight increase. Stop the strength block at 27 minutes.

Alternatives replace an exercise; they are not extra exercises. Choose a variation in the Log tab and keep it for several weeks to track progress. Weights are tracked separately for each exercise. For dumbbells, enter the weight of one dumbbell consistently.
